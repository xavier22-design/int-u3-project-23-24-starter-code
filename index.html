Your Triple T Story

You are Tung Tung Tung Sahur lost in the forest. You see a waterfall off of the path in the distance.

You make it to the waterfall but a foe approaches, it's Tralalero Tralala and he is hungry

You continue on the path and you stumble upon an injured Brr Brr Patapim

You try your hardest to defeat him, but he is too strong. You are defeated. R.I.P. Triple T.

You run away but he is too fast. You think all hope is lost but then out of the air comes Bombardino Crocodilo. You are saved and escape.

You help Brr Brr Patapim and you two live a long and happy life together.

You leave Brr Brr Patapim behind and escape the forest. You move on and get married but you spend everyday wondering what could have been if you had just helped.
